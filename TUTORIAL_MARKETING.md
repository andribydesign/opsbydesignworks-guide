# Marketing Team Operational Guide
## By Design Works — Lead Management System

Welcome to the **By Design Works Lead System** (`https://ops.bydesignworks.com`).  
This guide explains your daily workflow step-by-step using the **exact buttons, cards, and words** shown on your screen.

---

### The #1 Rule to Remember
> **Every open lead must always have a PIC (Owner), a Next Action, and a Due Date.**  
> The system will not allow any lead to be left blank or forgotten.

---

## 1. How to Log In
1. Open your browser and go to: **[https://ops.bydesignworks.com](https://ops.bydesignworks.com)**
2. Click **"Sign in with Google"**.
3. Choose your official company Google account (`yourname@bydesignworks.com`).
4. You will automatically land on your personal **MY DAY** page.

---

## 2. Your Daily Cockpit — `MY DAY`
Every morning and throughout the day, open **MY DAY** in the top navigation bar:

* **Requires First Response:** Shows fresh inbound enquiries that have not yet received a reply. Your goal is to reply on WhatsApp within **15 working minutes**.
* **Pending Qualification:** Leads where you have sent the first message, waiting for client replies to the 6 questions.
* **Pending Handover:** Consultations you booked where the assigned Interior Designer has not yet completed their 2-step checklist.

---

## 3. Creating a New Lead (WhatsApp, Instagram, Walk-in, Referral)

*Note: Enquiries submitted through the website form on `bydesignworks.com` appear automatically in the system! You only need to create leads that come from direct WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, or walk-ins.*

1. Click the black **+ NEW LEAD** button at the top right of the navigation bar.
2. In the search box **"Check for an existing client first"**, type the client's mobile number (e.g. `91234567`) and click **CHECK**.
   * If the client already exists, the system will warn you to prevent duplicates.
   * If new, continue filling the form below.
3. Fill in the **Lead details**:
   * **Client name:** Full name of the client (e.g. `Tan Boon Kiat`).
   * **Mobile / WhatsApp:** E.164 phone number (e.g. `+65 8812 3456`).
   * **Original source:** Select where they found By Design Works (`Website Form`, `Instagram / SeanBD`, `Meta Ads`, `Referral`, etc.).
   * **Current conversation channel:** Where you are chatting with them (`WhatsApp`, `Instagram DM`, `Phone Call`).
   * **Property / project:** Property name (e.g. `Skyline Residences` or `Punggol Northshore`).
   * **Category:** Choose `New BTO`, `Resale HDB`, `New Condo`, `Resale Condo`, or `Landed`.
   * **Unit:** Enter layout (e.g. `4-room`, `3-bedroom`, `Penthouse`).
   * **Client's first message:** Paste or summarise what they wrote.
   * **PIC — Marketing:** Select your name (e.g. `Aya (Marketing)` or `Yana (Marketing)`).
4. Click **CREATE LEAD**.

---

## 4. Stopping the SLA Clock — Logging First Response

When a new lead arrives, the green SLA clock starts ticking.

1. Reply to the client on WhatsApp with the standard greeting and the 6 qualification questions.
2. Open the lead page in the system.
3. In the **First response** card on the right, click **MARK RESPONDED**.
4. *(Optional)* Add a quick note (e.g. *"Greeted on WhatsApp, sent 6 questions"*).
5. Click **SAVE RESPONSE**.
   * ✅ The green badge will lock in your response time (e.g. `10m (SLA Met)`).

---

## 5. Saving the 6 Profile Questions & Qualifying the Lead

Once the client replies to your questions on WhatsApp:

1. Scroll to the **Lead Qualification Profile** card on the lead page.
2. Fill in the 6 fields:
   * **1. Property Category:** e.g. `Resale Condo`
   * **2. Unit Type:** e.g. `4-room`
   * **3. Property Name / Address:** e.g. `Skyline Residences #12-04`
   * **4. Estimated Start Date / Key Collection:** e.g. `Nov 2026`
   * **5. Renovation Budget (SGD):** e.g. `75000` *(Numbers only)*
   * **6. Scope of Work:** e.g. `Full overhaul, hacking kitchen wall, custom carpentry in master and living room.`
3. Click **SAVE PROFILE**.
4. In the **Qualification Decision** box, choose one of the three options:
   * **Qualified:** Client has a valid property, realistic budget (≥$30k), and timeline within 12 months.
   * **Nurture:** Key collection is >12 months away. Set a **Recontact Date** (e.g. 3 months before keys).
   * **Unqualified:** Budget under $30k, commercial scope outside BD focus, or client unresponsive after 3 follow-ups.
5. Click **CONFIRM QUALIFICATION**.
   * The status badge at the top changes to **QUALIFIED**.

---

## 6. Booking Meeting 1 (Consultation) & Assigning an Interior Designer

Once the client is qualified and agrees to meet:

1. Find the card titled **Book Consultation & Assign Designer**.
2. Fill in the unified form:
   * **Meeting Date & Time:** Select the date and time agreed with the client (e.g. `23 Sept 2026, 14:00`).
   * **Location:** Choose `Showroom`, `Client's Home / Site`, or `Virtual / Zoom`.
   * **Assigned Interior Designer:** Pick the designer from the dropdown (e.g. `Archie (ID / Drafter)`).
   * **Notes / Focus:** Any special client requests (e.g. *"Client loves Japandi style, prefers dark wood accents"*).
3. Click **SCHEDULE CONSULTATION**.
   * ✅ The lead stage moves to **APPOINTMENT BOOKED**.
   * ✅ The assigned Interior Designer receives an instant notification on their Telegram with appointment details and client notes.
   * ✅ The **Handover SLA clock** starts ticking (designer must confirm before the appointment).

---

## 7. Handover Follow-Up: Marketing's Job
After scheduling:
* You remain the **PIC (Owner)** until the Interior Designer confirms the handover checklist.
* Monitor the **Handover SLA** badge on the lead page:
  * If the badge turns amber or red, ping the designer on WhatsApp/Slack:
    > *"Hi Archie, please confirm Step 1 (WhatsApp group) and Step 2 (Accept Handover) for Tan Boon Kiat on the system."*
* Once the designer clicks Step 2, the PIC automatically moves from you to the Designer. Your job for this lead is now complete!

---

## Quick Reference Summary

| Stage | What You Do | Button to Click |
|---|---|---|
| **New Lead** | Message client on WhatsApp | Click **MARK RESPONDED** |
| **Questions Answered** | Record answers | Click **SAVE PROFILE** $\rightarrow$ **QUALIFIED** |
| **Consultation Agreed** | Choose date, time & designer | Click **SCHEDULE CONSULTATION** |
| **Handover** | Designer accepts project | Handover completes automatically |
