# Marketing Guide — BD Lead System

How the marketing team uses the lead system, step by step.

> **The one rule to remember**
> Every open lead always has **an owner, a next action, and a due date.**
> The system will not let you save a lead without them — that is how no client gets forgotten.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the lead system in your browser.
2. Click **Sign in with Google**.
3. Choose your **@bydesignworks.com** account.

There is no separate password — you sign in with your BD Google account.

| You see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The **Dashboard** | You're in. |
| *"That account can't sign in here"* | You picked a non-BD account. Try again with your @bydesignworks.com account. |
| *"No access yet"* | You're on the BD team, but your role doesn't include the lead system. Ask an admin. |

To sign out, click **Sign out** at the top right.

## 2. Your screens

The menu at the top has four pages, plus a gold **New lead** button on the right.

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | The whole team's picture: new leads, late replies, anything needing attention, the pipeline, upcoming appointments. |
| **My Day** | **Your own** to-do list. Start here every morning. |
| **Leads** | Every lead. Search and filter. |
| **Reports** | Which lead sources bring good clients, and why leads are lost. |

**Colours mean the same thing everywhere:**
🔴 **red** — late, fix it now · 🟠 **amber** — at risk, check it soon

## 3. Start of every day

### Step 1 — Open **My Day**

Work through it **top to bottom**:

| Section | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Next 3 days** | Appointments coming up. Check the ID and the client are confirmed. |
| **Overdue** | Things you promised and haven't done. Do them, or set a new date. |
| **Awaiting your first reply** | New clients waiting for an answer. **Reply within 1 working hour.** |
| **Handover not confirmed** | Leads you passed to an ID who hasn't taken over yet. Chase the ID. |
| **Due today** | Today's tasks that aren't late yet. |

If My Day is empty, you're up to date.

### Step 2 — Check the **Dashboard**

- Tiles along the top: **Awaiting reply** and **SLA breached** should be as low as possible.
- **Needs attention**: click any line to see those leads.

## 4. A lead from start to handover

```
① Lead enters the system
      ↓
② Reply to the client  →  Mark responded
      ↓
③ Ask the qualification questions  →  Qualification
      ↓
      ├─ Qualified    → ④ Book Meeting 1 → ⑤ Assign an ID → ⑥ Handover
      ├─ Nurture      → comes back on the re-contact date
      └─ Unqualified  → closed with a reason
```

### ① The lead enters the system

**Website enquiries** appear automatically. Each one is given an owner straight away (the person
set by your manager in Settings), with the task *"Respond and ask qualification questions"*.

**WhatsApp and Instagram enquiries** — you add them:

1. Click the gold **New lead** button.
2. **Check for an existing client first.** Type their phone, email or Instagram → **Check**.
   - Shown under *Possible existing client*? → click **New project for this client**.
     Their history stays together.
   - Not shown? → carry on below.
3. Fill in **Lead details**:

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| Client name | Their name |
| Mobile / WhatsApp | Their number |
| Email, Instagram handle | If you have them |
| **Original source** | Where they **found** BD — e.g. *Instagram / SeanBD*. ⚠️ **Cannot be changed later.** |
| Current conversation channel | Where you are **talking** now — e.g. *WhatsApp* |
| Property / project, Category, Unit, Location | e.g. *Punggol BTO*, *BTO*, *4-room*, *Punggol* |
| When did the client message? | **Leave blank** if they just messaged. If entering an older message, put the real time. |
| Owner | Normally you |

4. Click **Create lead**. The lead's page opens.

> **Source vs channel:** a client who found BD on SeanBD's Instagram and then moved to WhatsApp
> is still source **Instagram / SeanBD**. WhatsApp is only the channel.

---

### ② Reply to the client

1. Reply to the client on WhatsApp / Instagram as usual.
2. On the lead's page, find the **First response** box.
3. Check the next step — it's filled in for you:
   - **Next action:** *Chase qualification answers*
   - **Due:** tomorrow
   Change them if you need to.
4. Click **Mark responded**.

The response time is recorded and the lead moves to **Contacting**.

> ⏱ **Reply within 1 working hour** (currently Mon–Sat, 9:30am–6:30pm).
> Messages outside working hours start the clock at the next working period.

---

### ③ Qualify the lead

Ask the client the qualification questions:

- What type of unit is it?
- Which areas are you planning to renovate?
- When are you looking to start renovation?
- What budget are you looking at?
- When are you collecting your keys?
- When are you looking to confirm an ID?

Then, on the lead's page, in the **Qualification** box:

1. Fill in **Budget (S$)**, **Key collection**, **Renovation scope**, **Target ID confirmation**.
2. Tick **Price sensitive / quote matching** if they're comparing quotations. *(This is only a
   note — it does not reject the lead.)*
3. Choose the **Decision** — the form changes to match:

| Decision | The form asks for |
|---|---|
| **Qualified** | **Next action** (filled in: *Book Meeting 1*) and **Due** |
| **Nurture / Future** | **Re-contact on** (a date) and **What are we waiting for?** |
| **Unqualified** | **Unqualified reason** and a **Note** |

4. Click **Save qualification**.

**Budget under S$30,000 but you still think they're a good lead?** Choose Qualified — an amber
box asks for an **Override reason**, e.g. *"Referral from a signed client"*. That's allowed; it's
simply recorded.

**Client hasn't answered the questions yet?** Don't guess. See
[Chasing unanswered questions](#chasing-unanswered-qualification-questions).

Not sure which decision? See [section 5](#5-deciding-qualified-nurture-or-unqualified).

---

### ④ Book Meeting 1

On the lead's page, in the **Appointments** box:

1. **Book meeting 1** — date and time
2. **Location** — e.g. *BD Showroom, Ubi*
3. **With** — the ID, if you already know who
4. Click **Book**

The lead moves to **Appointment Booked**, and its next action becomes *Meeting 1*.

---

### ⑤ Assign an ID

In the **ID assignment and handover** box:

1. **Assign ID** — choose the interior designer
2. **Why this ID** — e.g. *available that day*, *style fit*
3. Click **Assign**

When choosing, consider availability, workload, the client's needs, the meeting date, and the
schedule of Indonesia design staff travelling to Singapore.

A **handover deadline** appears — whichever comes first:
- 24 working hours after you assign, or
- 12 hours before the appointment

---

### ⑥ Make sure the handover happens

> **Booking the appointment does not finish the handover.**
> Until the ID confirms, **you** are still responsible for the lead.

The same box shows three steps, normally done by the ID:

1. ☐ **WhatsApp group created** → **Confirm**
2. ☐ **Introduction sent** → **Confirm**
3. ☐ **Handover confirmed** → **Confirm** — *the ID now owns the lead*

If a handover is running late, it shows in **My Day → Handover not confirmed** and on the
**Dashboard → Handover overdue**. Message the ID.

After handover, **the ID runs the meetings**. You can still open the lead and see its progress.

## 5. Deciding: Qualified, Nurture or Unqualified

Not every enquiry is ready for an appointment.

### ✅ Qualified — book Meeting 1

Generally all of these:
- Scope is meaningful enough for BD
- Budget is realistic
- Client genuinely intends to renovate
- The project fits BD
- They want to appoint an ID within roughly **3 months**
- Their keys / renovation timeline makes sense
- No major warning signs

### 🕐 Nurture — good client, too early

- Keys come much later
- Renovation is many months or years away
- They'll only confirm an ID after collecting keys, and that's more than ~3 months away
- Still researching, not ready to appoint anyone

A nurture lead **must have a re-contact date**. It comes back to you on that date.

### ❌ Unqualified — close with a reason

- Very small project, odd jobs or repairs
- Budget generally below ~S$20–30k, or unrealistic for the scope
- Outside BD's service scope
- Irrelevant enquiry, spam or vendor
- Obvious price hunter

### Price hunters — look carefully

| Normal comparison client | Obvious price hunter |
|---|---|
| Talking to a few IDs — that's normal | Sends a long competitor BOQ and asks BD to copy and beat it |
| Interested in BD's design and service | Only interested in the lowest price |
| → Tick **Price sensitive**, qualify normally | → **Unqualified** — *Price shopping / quotation matching* |

### Nurture or Lost?

Ask: **is there a real future event to wait for?**
- **Yes** — keys, funds, a renovation date → **Nurture**, dated to that event
- **No** → close it

## 6. Everyday situations

### Chasing unanswered qualification questions

If the client hasn't answered, set the next chase in the **Next action** box at the bottom of
the lead's page, then click **Set**.

| Chase | When |
|---|---|
| Follow-up 1 | About 1 day after the enquiry |
| Follow-up 2 | About 2–3 days after |
| Follow-up 3 | About 7 days after |

Still no reply after follow-up 3? Choose **Nurture** with a re-contact date — **never just delete
or ignore** the lead.

> These follow-ups are **not automatic yet**. Set each one yourself in **Next action**.

### Quick reference

| Situation | Where to click |
|---|---|
| **Change what happens next** | Lead page → **Next action** → what happens next + **Due** → **Set** |
| **Close a lead** (client chose another ID, stopped replying…) | Lead page → **Close this lead** → **Lost reason** (+ note if *Other*) → **Mark lost** |
| **Client didn't turn up to a meeting** | **Appointments** → under that meeting choose **No-show** → set the next action → **Record outcome**. The lead goes back to *Qualified* so you can rebook. |
| **Client cancelled or rescheduled** | Same place → **Cancelled by client** or **Rescheduled** → next action → **Record outcome** |
| **Nurture lead due for re-contact** | Shows in **Needs attention**. Lead page → **Nurture** box → **Next re-contact** date → **Log re-contact attempt** |
| **Nurture lead never replies** | After 3 attempts, leave the date blank on the last one → the lead goes **dormant**. It's kept, just out of your list. |
| **A closed or dormant client gets in touch again** | Open their lead → **Reopen** box → next action + due → **Reopen lead**. Their history is kept, and the reply clock starts again. |
| **Same client, a different property** | **New lead** → **Check** → **New project for this client** |
| **Client asks not to be contacted** | Lead page → **Client** box → reason → **Do not contact** |
| **Find a lead** | **Leads** → search by name, lead code, phone or property. Buttons: **Open only**, **Mine**, and each stage. |
| **See which sources bring good clients** | **Reports** |

### Lost reasons

*No response · Chose another ID · Price · Design preference · Timeline · Location/project issue ·
Client not serious · Other (note required)*

### Unqualified reasons

*Budget too low · Scope too small · Odd job / repair · Unrealistic budget · Project not suitable ·
Price shopping / quotation matching · Timeline too far away · Client not serious · Spam / vendor ·
Other (note required)*

## 7. What marketing cannot do

Ask your **marketing manager** or an **admin** for these:

| Task | Who |
|---|---|
| Move a lead to a different owner | Marketing manager, admin |
| Close a lead as **Lost after the proposal was presented** | Marketing manager confirms it |
| Change who receives website leads by default | Marketing manager, admin |
| Change rules (reply time, working hours, budget threshold) | Admin |
| Give someone access to the system | Admin |

## 8. Messages you might see

Messages appear in a **red box at the top** of the page. Nothing is saved when you see one —
fix it and try again.

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Next action cannot be blank* / *A next action needs a due date* | Fill in both the next action and the due date. |
| *A nurture lead needs a re-contact date* | Choose when to contact them again. |
| *Budget is below the S$30,000 threshold. Qualifying anyway needs an override reason* | Add a reason in the amber box, or choose Nurture / Unqualified. |
| *Reason "Other" requires a note* | Write a short note explaining. |
| *Choose an unqualified reason from the list* | Pick one of the listed reasons. |
| *Losses from Proposal Presented onward need a marketing manager's confirmation* | Ask your marketing manager to close it. |
| *Choose the next re-contact date. Attempt 1 of 3* | Pick the next date. Only the last attempt can be left blank. |
| *This lead has already been marked as responded* | Nothing to do — it was already recorded. |
| *Cannot move a lead from … to …* | That step isn't allowed from where the lead is now. Ask your manager if unsure. |
| *That could not be saved. Nothing was changed* | Try again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin. |

## 9. Words used in the system

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lead** | One renovation project enquiry |
| **ID** | Interior designer |
| **Owner** | The person responsible for the lead right now |
| **Next action** | The very next thing that must happen, with a due date |
| **Source** | Where the client first found BD. Never changes. |
| **Channel** | Where you're currently talking to the client |
| **SLA** | The 1-working-hour limit to reply to a new lead |
| **Handover** | Passing a lead to the ID: WhatsApp group → introduction → confirmed |
| **Meeting 1 / 2 / 3** | Discovery · layout, 3D and quotation · revisions and closing |
| **Nurture** | A good client who is too early — comes back on a re-contact date |
| **Dormant** | A nurture lead that stopped replying. Kept, but out of your daily list. |
| **Unqualified** | Not a fit for BD — closed with a reason |
| **Lost** | Was a real opportunity, but didn't go ahead — closed with a reason |
| **Pipeline** | New → Contacting → Qualified → Appointment Booked → Consultation → Proposal → Closing → Signed |
