# Interior Designer (ID) Operational Guide
## By Design Works — Lead Management System

Welcome to the **By Design Works Lead System** (`https://ops.bydesignworks.com`).  
This guide explains your daily workflow step-by-step using the **exact buttons, cards, and words** shown on your screen.

---

### The #1 Rule to Remember
> **Every open lead must always have a Next Action and a Due Date.**  
> Whenever you complete a meeting or talk to a client, record what happens next (e.g. *"Deliver 3D Moodboard"* or *"Follow up on quotation"*) with a realistic deadline.

---

## 1. Setting Up Your Telegram Alerts (One-Time Setup — Takes 10 Seconds)
All your new consultation appointments, handover requests, and follow-up reminders will be sent directly to your phone via Telegram.

1. Open Telegram on your phone or computer.
2. Search for: **`@leadsbydesign_bot`**
3. Tap **Start** (or send `/start`).
4. The bot will automatically recognize you and reply:
   > *"👋 Welcome, [Your Name]! Your Telegram account is successfully linked to By Design Works Lead System. Role: Interior Designer"*
5. You are all set! You will now receive instant alerts whenever Marketing books an appointment for you.

---

## 2. How to Log In
1. Go to: **[https://ops.bydesignworks.com](https://ops.bydesignworks.com)**
2. Click **"Sign in with Google"**.
3. Choose your company Google account (`yourname@bydesignworks.com`).
4. You will land on your personalized **MY DAY** dashboard.

---

## 3. When a New Consultation is Assigned to You
When Marketing schedules an appointment for you, you will receive a Telegram message with:
* Client name & property details (e.g. *Tan Boon Kiat · Skyline Residences 4-room*).
* Appointment date, time, and location (e.g. *23 Sept 2026, 14:00 at BD Showroom*).
* Direct link to open the lead.

---

## 4. The 2-Step Handover Checklist (Mandatory)
Before your consultation meeting takes place, you must complete the **2-Step Handover Checklist** on the lead page:

### Step 1: Create the WhatsApp Group & Send Introduction
1. On your phone, create a WhatsApp group with:
   * **The Client**
   * **Yourself**
   * **The Marketing PIC** (e.g. Aya or Yana)
2. Send the standard welcome/introduction message in the group.
3. Open the lead page on the website.
4. Under the **Handover Checklist** card, click:  
   👉 **CONFIRM STEP 1 (GROUP INTRO)**
   * ✅ A green checkmark `✓` will appear next to Step 1.

### Step 2: Accept the Handover (Transfer Ownership)
1. Review the client's property type, budget, scope of work, and floor plan.
2. Click:  
   👉 **CONFIRM STEP 2 (ACCEPT HANDOVER)**
   * ✅ A green checkmark `✓` will appear next to Step 2.
   * ✅ **The PIC (Owner) of the lead is now transferred to YOU.**
   * You now have full permission to record meetings, update proposals, and close the deal.

---

## 5. Meeting 1 (Consultation) — Recording the Outcome

After you meet the client at the showroom, home, or Zoom:

1. Open the lead page.
2. Scroll to the **Meeting 1 (Consultation)** card.
3. Click **MARK HELD**:
   * Enter the date and time the meeting took place.
4. Select the **Outcome**:
   * **Proceeding to Proposal:** The client is keen. You are preparing drawings or quotation. Stage advances to `PROPOSAL PRESENTED`.
   * **Client Considering:** The client needs time to discuss with family. (You have up to 7 days to follow up).
   * **Lost:** The client decided not to proceed.
5. Fill in the **Next Action & Due Date**:
   * **Next Action:** Type your next task (e.g. *"Deliver 3D Moodboard & Detailed Quotation"* or *"WhatsApp client to confirm revision meeting"*).
   * **Due Date:** Select when you promise to finish this action.
6. Click **UPDATE MEETING**.

---

## 6. Scheduling Subsequent Meetings (Meeting 2 / 3+)

When scheduling a follow-up presentation or revision review:

1. On the lead page, scroll to the **Appointments** section.
2. Click **SCHEDULE NEXT MEETING**.
3. Choose the date, time, and location.
4. Set the purpose (e.g. *"3D Layout Presentation"* or *"Quotation Review"*).
5. Click **SAVE APPOINTMENT**.

---

## 7. Winning the Deal — Marking Signed (Revenue Won)

When the client approves the quotation and signs the renovation contract:

1. Open the lead page.
2. Scroll to the **Contract Signing** card.
3. Click **MARK SIGNED**.
4. Enter the details:
   * **Final Contract Value (SGD):** Enter the exact signed amount in dollars (e.g. `78500`).
   * **Contract Signed Date:** Today's date.
5. Click **LOCK SIGNED DEAL**.
   * ✅ The lead status permanently moves to **SIGNED (WON)**.
   * ✅ The revenue is credited to you and logged in the company commercial reports!

---

## 8. What Happens if a Client Drops Out at Proposal Stage?
* Once a lead has reached the **Proposal** stage, closing the deal as **Lost** requires a **Marketing Manager or Admin approval**.
* Simply click **Close as Lost**, select the reason (e.g. *Over budget*, *Chose another interior firm*), and submit.
* Your Manager will review and sign off on the close.
