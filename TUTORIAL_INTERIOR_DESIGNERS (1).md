# Interior Designer (ID) Operational Guide
## By Design Works — Lead Management System & Quotation System

Welcome to the **By Design Works Lead System** (`https://ops.bydesignworks.com`).  
This guide explains your daily workflow step-by-step using the **exact buttons, cards, and words** shown on your screen.

---

### The #1 Rule to Remember
> **Every open lead must always have a Next Action and a Due Date.**  
> Whenever you complete a meeting or talk to a client, record what happens next (e.g. *"Deliver 3D Moodboard"* or *"Follow up on quotation"*) with a realistic deadline.

---

## 1. Setting Up Your Telegram Alerts (One-Time Setup — Takes 10 Seconds)
All your new consultation appointments, handover requests, and follow-up reminders will be sent directly to your phone via Telegram.

1. Open Telegram on your phone or computer.
2. Search for: **`@leadsbydesign_bot`**
3. Tap **Start** (or send `/start`).
4. The bot will automatically recognize you and reply:
   > *"👋 Welcome, [Your Name]! Your Telegram account is successfully linked to By Design Works Lead System. Role: Interior Designer"*
5. You are all set! You will now receive instant alerts whenever Marketing books an appointment for you.

> **Not linked yet?** You still get the alerts in the **operations Telegram group**, where you are tagged by your @username, with a line saying you have not linked the bot. Link it (steps above) to also get every alert **privately** — from your next alert onwards, automatically.

## 2. How to Log In
1. Go to: **[https://ops.bydesignworks.com](https://ops.bydesignworks.com)**
2. Click **"Sign in with Google"**.
3. Choose your company Google account (`yourname@bydesignworks.com`).
4. You land on the **Operations Hub**. Click **Enter Lead System** — it opens your personalized **MY DAY** dashboard.

Your menu is **MY DAY · LEADS · QUOTATIONS · ACTIVITY**.

**MY DAY** shows only your own work:
* Your appointments in the next 3 days, overdue follow-ups, leads with no next action, handovers waiting on you, and what is due today.
* **My quotations:** quotes you prepared that are still in progress — *Revision required* at the top.
* **My SLA & compliance:** your own score, with every issue listed — click one to fix it.
* **My performance:** your active leads, proposals, and what you signed this month.

**LEADS** lets you look up any client read-only (for past notes and repeat clients); you can only
change leads you own or are assigned to. **ACTIVITY** shows the history of your leads.

## 3. When a New Consultation is Assigned to You
When Marketing books Meeting 1 and picks you as the designer, you get a **CONSULTATION & HANDOVER ASSIGNED** Telegram alert (in the group, and privately if you linked the bot) with:
* Client name, contact (WhatsApp link) & property details (e.g. *Tan Boon Kiat · Skyline Residences 4-room*).
* Budget, key collection, Meeting 1 date, time and location, and the floor plan link.
* The **handover checklist** and its **deadline**, plus a direct link to the lead.

### Your first job is the handover — and it has a deadline
On the lead page (and in **MY DAY → handovers waiting on you**) your next action is now:

> **Complete handover: WhatsApp group, client joined, intro, calendar — then confirm**
> Due: the **handover deadline** · Waiting on: **ID (you)**

The handover deadline is **whichever comes first**:
* **24 working hours after you were assigned** — working hours are **Mon–Sat, 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM** (Sundays and public holidays don't count), or
* **12 hours before Meeting 1**.

*Example:* assigned **Fri 10:39 AM**, Meeting 1 on **Sun 11 Oct 3:00 PM** → Fri 10:39–6:30 PM (7 h 51 m) + Sat 9:30–6:30 (9 h) + Mon 9:30 AM → **4:39 PM** = 24 working hours. That is earlier than *12 hours before the meeting* (Sun 3:00 AM), so the deadline is **Mon 4:39 PM**.

If the deadline passes before you confirm Step 2, the lead shows as **overdue** and is flagged to management (Needs Attention and the SLA report).

## 4. The 2-Step Handover Checklist (Mandatory)
Before your consultation meeting takes place, you must complete the **2-Step Handover Checklist** on the lead page:

### Step 1: WhatsApp Group, Client Joined, Intro Sent, Meeting in Your Calendar
1. On your phone, create a WhatsApp group with:
   * **The Client**
   * **Yourself**
   * **The Marketing PIC** (e.g. Aya or Yana)
2. Make sure the **client has joined** the group.
3. Send the standard welcome/introduction message in the group.
4. Add **Meeting 1** (date, time, location) to your own calendar.
5. Open the lead page on the website. Under the **Handover Checklist** card, click **Confirm** beside:  
   👉 **1. WhatsApp group created, client has joined, intro sent, meeting added to calendar**
   * ✅ A green checkmark `✓` will appear next to Step 1.

### Step 2: Accept the Handover (Transfer Ownership)
1. Review the client's property type, budget, scope of work, and floor plan.
2. Under the **Handover Checklist** card, click **Confirm** beside:  
   👉 **2. Handover confirmed — PIC moves to the ID**
   * ✅ A green checkmark `✓` will appear next to Step 2.
   * ✅ **The PIC (Owner) of the lead is now transferred to YOU.**
   * You now have full permission to record meetings, update proposals, and close the deal.
   * 📣 The Telegram group gets a **HANDOVER COMPLETED** message tagging you and the Marketing PIC (and you both get it privately if you have linked the bot).
   * 🔁 Your **next action switches automatically** to **"Attend Meeting 1 & Record Outcome"**, due on the follow-up date Marketing chose when booking — or, if they left it empty, the **next business day after the meeting at 9:30 AM**. The lead's **History** shows a line explaining where each automatic date came from.

## 5. Meeting 1 (Consultation) — Recording the Outcome

After you meet the client at the showroom, home, or Zoom — and **before the due date of "Attend Meeting 1 & Record Outcome"**:

1. Open the lead page.
2. Scroll to the **Meeting 1 (Consultation)** card.
3. Click **MARK HELD**:
   * Enter the date and time the meeting took place.
4. Select the **Outcome**:
   * **Proceeding to Proposal:** The client is keen. You are preparing drawings or quotation. Stage advances to `PROPOSAL PRESENTED`.
   * **Client Considering:** The client needs time to discuss with family. (You have up to 7 days to follow up).
   * **Lost:** The client decided not to proceed.
5. Fill in the **Next Action & Due Date**:
   * **Next Action:** Type your next task (e.g. *"Deliver 3D Moodboard & Detailed Quotation"* or *"WhatsApp client to confirm revision meeting"*).
   * **Due Date:** Select when you promise to finish this action.
6. Click **UPDATE MEETING**.

---

## 6. Scheduling Subsequent Meetings (Meeting 2 / 3+)

When scheduling a follow-up presentation or revision review:

1. On the lead page, scroll to the **Appointments** section.
2. Click **SCHEDULE NEXT MEETING**.
3. Choose the date, time, and location.
4. Set the purpose (e.g. *"3D Layout Presentation"* or *"Quotation Review"*).
5. Click **SAVE APPOINTMENT**.

---

## 7. Winning the Deal — Marking Signed (Revenue Won)

When the client approves the quotation and signs the renovation contract:

1. Open the lead page.
2. Scroll to the **Contract Signing** card.
3. Click **MARK SIGNED**.
4. Enter the details:
   * **Final Contract Value (SGD):** Enter the exact signed amount in dollars (e.g. `78500`).
   * **Contract Signed Date:** Today's date.
5. Click **LOCK SIGNED DEAL**.
   * ✅ The lead status permanently moves to **SIGNED (WON)**.
   * ✅ The revenue is credited to you and logged in the company commercial reports!

---

## 8. What Happens if a Client Drops Out at Proposal Stage?
* Once a lead has reached the **Proposal** stage, closing the deal as **Lost** requires a **Marketing Manager or Admin approval**.
* Simply click **Close as Lost**, select the reason (e.g. *Over budget*, *Chose another interior firm*), and submit.
* Your Manager will review and sign off on the close.

## 9. The Quotation System — Preparing Quotes & Variation Orders

Open it from the top menu: **QUOTATIONS**. The list shows every quote with its number (e.g. `Q-2026-0012`), version, status, client and total. The cards at the top (**DRAFTS · READY FOR REVIEW · PRESENTED TO CLIENT · SIGNED & PAID**) filter the list. Your own unfinished quotes also appear on **MY DAY → My quotations**.

> **What you will not see:** internal cost, gross profit (GP) and margin % are for management only. A client's full **NRIC** is shown only to Sean and Sharen; everyone else sees *Restricted*. You can still type the NRIC when you create the quote.

### 9.1 Who does what

| Step | You (ID) | Manager (Sean / Sharen / Marketing Manager) |
|---|---|---|
| Create the quote, build the scope, revise | ✅ | ✅ |
| Submit for review | ✅ | — |
| Approve, or return with notes | — | ✅ |
| Mark presented (locks the version) | — | ✅ |
| Confirm signed & paid, attach the signed contract | — | ✅ |
| Create a Variation Order (VO) and submit it | ✅ | ✅ |
| Approve, present and accept a VO; attach the signed VO | — | ✅ |
| Void a VO the client declined | ✅ (your own VO) | ✅ |

### 9.2 Create a new quotation
1. **QUOTATIONS → + New Quotation**.
2. Fill in the client details: **Client name, NRIC, phone, email, project address**.
3. Choose the **Property Type**. This decides which **price list** the quote uses, so pick it carefully:

   | Property Type | Price list used |
   |---|---|
   | BTO 8.5Ft ceilin height | BTO |
   | Resale BTO 8.5ft · Resale HDB 8.5 · HDB Highlest story 9ft | Resale HDB |
   | New Condo (9ft) · Resale Condo (9ft) | Condo |
   | Landed (9ft) | Landed |
   | Commercial (9ft) | Commercial |

4. Set **GST**, the **Renovation scope** (Whole house, Kitchen & baths, Partial, Makeover), any **Known exclusions** and **Client-supplied items**.
5. Optionally pick a **Template** (e.g. *HDB BTO*, *Condo overhaul*). Its lines are priced automatically from the price list in step 3.
6. Click **INITIALIZE QUOTE & OPEN BUILDER**.

### 9.3 Build the scope (Scope Builder)
* Each trade has its own section: Carpentry, Masonry Works, Vinyl Works, Countertop, Plumbing, Ceiling/Cornice, Painting, Miscellaneous and so on. Click **+ Add Item to [section]** and choose an item from the Master Library. The unit price fills in from the property's price list.
* **Something not in the library?** Choose **-- Custom Work (Type New Description) --** and type it in. To get a proper library rate for next time, go to **QUOTATIONS → Pending Prices → + SUBMIT CUSTOM TRADE QUOTE** and name the quote it is for. Each request then shows where it went: approved *one-off for Q-xxxx* or *added to the Master Library*.
* **Changing a standard price** needs a **Reason (Required)**. Your manager sees it during review.
* **📦 LOAD TEMPLATE** adds a template's lines at any time. Discount lines (**+Volume Discount**, **+Competitive Match**, **+Package Goodwill**) are at the bottom.
* The draft **auto-saves every 30 seconds**. Click **SAVE DRAFT** to save now.

### 9.4 Submit for review
1. Click **SUBMIT FOR REVIEW →** (in the builder) or **SUBMIT REVIEW →** (on the quote page).
2. The status becomes **Ready for review** and the managers get a Telegram alert.
3. On the lead, set your **Next action** to *"Proposal submitted to Sharen for review"* with **Waiting on: Manager**.
4. The manager then either:
   * **Approves** the quote. You get a Telegram alert. Status: **Approved**.
   * **Returns it with notes**. Status: **Revision required**, shown at the top of *My quotations*. Read the notes, fix the scope, and submit again.

### 9.5 Present the PDF to the client
* **PDF Preview / Client PDF** opens the client proposal. Each trade shows its **Subtotal**, and pages are numbered *Page X of Y*.
* To save or print, use **Print → Save as PDF** in Chrome or Edge, and **untick "Headers and footers"** so the website address isn't printed.
* After you present it, a manager clicks **MARK PRESENTED (LOCK V1)**. The version is then locked.

### 9.6 Revisions and version history
* Client wants changes after seeing it? Click **+ REVISE (V2)**. A new version is created and the old one is kept.
* **Version history** on the quote page lists every version with its status and total. **View PDF** opens any version's PDF; **Compare with v1** (on V2) shows exactly what was added, removed or changed between the two versions.

### 9.7 Correcting client details
Click **✎ Edit Details** on the quote page, update the name, phone, email, address, property type or scope, then **Save Changes**. The NRIC is kept even though you can't see it.

### 9.8 When the client signs
* A manager confirms **Signed & Paid** with the deposit or payment reference, and attaches the signed contract (PDF or photo). It then appears as **View Signed Contract** on the quote.
* On the lead, click **MARK SIGNED** with the final contract value (see Section 7).

### 9.9 Variation Orders (VO) — changes after signing
1. On the signed quote, click **+ CREATE VARIATION ORDER**. VO numbers run VO01, VO02 and so on.
2. Add as many lines as you need with **+ ADD ADDITION** (extra work) and **+ CUSTOM DEDUCTION** (removed work). Each line has its description, quantity and rate.
3. **Variation Order Payment Terms:** pick a preset (*50% / 50% Standard*, *100% Upfront*, *With Contract*, *3rd Milestone*, *Upon Final Handover*) or **Custom Schedule…**
4. **Terms & Conditions:** the VO has its own terms. Edit them here, or use **Reset to Standard Clauses**.
5. Click **SAVE DRAFT** to keep working, or **SAVE & SUBMIT FOR MANAGER REVIEW** when it is ready. Submitting always saves every line first.
6. The manager approves the VO (or returns it with *Manager requested revisions*), presents it, and confirms acceptance with the client's signed VO attached.
7. **The VO's starting figure includes earlier VOs.** From VO02 onwards, the VO shows **Previously Approved VOs** and **Contract Sum Prior to VO0X**: the original contract plus the VOs already accepted.
8. **Client decides not to go ahead?** Open the VO → **Cancel / void this VO**, type the reason (e.g. *"Client decided not to proceed after presentation"*) and confirm. The VO stays on record, its PDF is marked **VOID**, and it never counts in the contract sum. This **cannot be undone**, and an accepted VO cannot be voided.

### 9.10 Linking a quote to its lead
When the link is switched on by management (Settings), the quote page shows **Linked lead:**. Enter the lead code (e.g. `BD-2026-0003`) and click **Link**. The lead then shows the quote number, version, value and status, and the lead's value follows the latest version automatically. You never re-type the amount.
